**aitower** is a local-first desktop workbench for long-form writing, research synthesis, and visual thinking. It reads and writes directly to your Obsidian vault so writing and diagramming live in one window.

## Human-Crafted Hybrid

Instead of acting as an assembly-line content generator, aitower serves as a thinking scaffold:

- **You steer the writing**: Use AI to search across notes, surface counter-intuitive angles, expand outlines, and build comparison matrices while keeping your own voice.
- **Diagrams make logic visible**: Sketch hand-drawn architecture and flowcharts on an Excalidraw-powered board instead of generating generic plastic AI art.

## Two Core Modules

1. **Notes Workspace**: Wired directly to your Obsidian vault with CodeMirror 6 dual-mode editing (Live Preview / Source / Read), `[[Wikilink]]` completion, and native `.canvas` / `.excalidraw` support.
2. **Studio Board**: An infinite hand-drawn canvas with personal and official asset drawers, frames, templates, image tools, and one-click export & insertion at your cursor.
