The Studio dock includes an **Asset library** and **Board templates** so your diagrams keep a consistent hand-crafted look without drawing every shape from scratch.

## Asset Library: Mine vs. Official

Open the Asset library in the left dock to switch between two tabs:

- **Official**: Built-in architecture components, hand-drawn icons, sticky tapes, and device wireframes ready to drag onto the canvas.
- **Mine**: Your personal collection of saved shapes, parts, and images, filterable by **Freq / Parts / Imgs** and searchable by keyword.

> Assets and boards sync cleanly on disk so you can maintain one cohesive visual language across articles.

## Board Templates (Local / Cloud)

Switch to **Board templates** in the dock to pick from preset layouts:

| Template | Best For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Brainstorm** | Sticky-note ideation and clustering |
| **Mind map** | Branching topics and concept maps |
| **Task board** | Tracking to-do, in-progress, and done states |
| **User journey** | Mapping stages and pain points |
| **Flowchart** | Step-by-step flows and decision branches |
| **Roadmap** | Planning milestones and timelines |
| **Blank board** | Clean independent canvas |

Save any frequently used layout as a local template to reuse across series or recurring reviews.
