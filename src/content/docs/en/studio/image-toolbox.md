Before publishing long-form articles or social cards, images often need resizing, compression, or background cleanup. aitower builds an **Image Toolbox** directly into the Studio dock.

## Included Tools

| Tool | What It Does | Common Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Batch compression** | Tune quality and output format | Keep article images lightweight |
| **Batch resize** | Scale by ratio or use social presets | Prep visuals for multi-channel publishing |
| **Batch crop** | Crop consistently to `1:1`, `4:3`, `16:9`, or `3:4` | Keep card series visually uniform |
| **Smart cutout** | AI subject isolation on complex backgrounds | Turn screenshots/photos into clean board stickers |
| **Image upscaling** | `2x / 4x` super-resolution for soft images | Sharpen low-res screenshots and references |

## Workflow

1. Open **Image tools** from the Studio dock.
2. Select a tool from the left list.
3. **Add images**: Drag local PNG, JPG, or WebP files onto the dropzone, or click **From notes** to pick images already stored in your vault.
4. Configure output parameters on the right panel and click **Start processing**.
5. Save processed files straight back into your Obsidian vault for immediate insertion.
