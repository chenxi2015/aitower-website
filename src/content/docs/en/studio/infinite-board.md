The **Studio** workspace is an infinite hand-drawn canvas tailored for system architecture diagrams, flowcharts, knowledge cards, and visual explainers.

## Aspect Ratios & Board Navigation

The top toolbar lets you switch canvas aspect ratios depending on where you plan to publish:

- **Auto**: Expands freely for large architecture maps and brainstorming.
- **3:4 / 9:16**: Ideal for vertical social cards and mobile-first layouts.
- **16:9**: Built for widescreen blog figures, slides, and video covers.

From the left dock, you can switch between:

- **Board list**: Browse and manage boards across your workspace.
- **Board frames**: Jump straight to named frames (`Goals`, `User Flow`, `Decisions`, etc.) within a complex board.
- **Board templates**: Start faster with local or cloud preset structures.

## Bottom Canvas Controls

The bottom bar provides quick zoom controls, fit-to-screen, grid snapping, canvas lock, and undo/redo actions.
