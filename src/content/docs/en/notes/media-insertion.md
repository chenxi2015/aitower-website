Conventional illustrating breaks your flow: switch to a drawing app, export to desktop, drag into an assets folder, and type the image link manually. aitower collapses that into a single loop.

## One-Click Insert from the Studio Board

1. **Finalize on the board**: Finish your architecture diagram, flowchart, or quote card on the Studio canvas or within a specific frame.
2. **Export to vault assets**: aitower exports a crisp **PNG** or **SVG** (including 2x resolution) directly into your Obsidian vault's assets folder.
3. **Stamp at cursor**: The standard Markdown reference is inserted automatically where you were typing:

```markdown
![[retry-flow.png]]
```

## Drag-to-Place Images & Live Preview

- **Direct image placement**: Drag local images—or outputs processed in the [Image Toolbox](/en/docs/studio/image-toolbox)—straight into your draft.
- **Immediate visual rhythm**: In Live Preview mode, embedded figures render inline so you can review pacing between prose and diagrams in real time.
