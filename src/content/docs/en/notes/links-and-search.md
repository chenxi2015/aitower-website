Once an Obsidian vault grows, the hardest part is not capturing notes—it is reconnecting scattered ideas across folders when drafting a new article.

## Wikilink Completion

Type `[[` inside the Markdown editor to get live suggestions across notes, studio boards, and image assets in your vault, preserving full compatibility with Obsidian's link syntax.

## Retrieval Across `.canvas` and Diagram Assets

Beyond basic filename filtering, aitower's search and AI retrieval pipeline works across multiple local formats:

- **Markdown notes (`.md`)**: Fast filename filtering and semantic context retrieval.
- **Obsidian Canvas (`.canvas`)**: Reads card structures and connections across boards.
- **Excalidraw boards (`.excalidraw`)**: Includes visual diagrams as first-class knowledge assets.

## Brainstorming Across Folders with the Agent

When stuck on an outline, ask the right-hand Agent panel to cross-reference folders—for example:

```text
Spark ideas from my "Learning" and "Creator" notes and suggest counter-intuitive angles.
```

The Agent searches and reads relevant notes, shows each step transparently in the conversation trace, and lets you decide what enters your draft.
