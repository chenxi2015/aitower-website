Instead of copying and pasting between your editor and external browser tabs, aitower embeds the AI Agent directly on the right side of the workbench.

## Context Awareness

The Agent automatically understands what you are currently working on:

- **Active note**: The Markdown document and section under your cursor.
- **Referenced notes**: Vault notes and backlinks retrieved during search.
- **Board elements**: Shapes, frames, and layout state on the active Studio board.

## Everyday Collaboration

- **Brainstorm angles**: Cross-reference notes across folders to surface counter-intuitive theses.
- **Structured expansion**: Turn rough bullet points into clean outlines or build comparison matrices.
- **Cross-note synthesis**: Read multiple research notes and `.canvas` boards together to distill key takeaways.

## Concurrent Sessions Without Cross-Talk

Run a research or editing session on your draft while another Agent session drafts diagrams on the Studio board. Background sessions stream silently so you can jump in whenever ready without losing context.
