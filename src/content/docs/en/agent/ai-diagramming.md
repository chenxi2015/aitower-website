Generic image generators produce flat bitmaps with garbled labels that cannot be updated when your logic changes. aitower uses a **human-crafted hybrid workflow**: AI drafts the structure, and you refine the details.

## The Three-Step Loop

![aitower three-step illustration loop](/docs/illustration-loop-en.svg)

### 1. Summon
While writing, trigger **"Illustrate this section"** or ask the side-panel Agent in plain language (for example, *"Sketch a hand-drawn system architecture for request → retry → idempotent gateway"*).

### 2. Design on the Board
The Agent reads relevant vault notes and drafts aligned shapes, connector arrows, and sticky notes directly on the Studio board. Everything generated is **fully editable**—move nodes, swap icons from the Asset library, or tweak colors right on the canvas.

### 3. Insert at Cursor
Export and insert with one click: a crisp PNG/SVG lands in your vault assets folder, and the Markdown reference is stamped right at your cursor.
