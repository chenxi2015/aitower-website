These three workflows show how Notes, Studio, and the embedded Agent work together in daily practice.

## 1. Technical Deep Dives & Post-Mortems

1. **Synthesize notes**: Open incident reviews, system notes, or `.canvas` drafts and ask the Agent to extract core tensions and counter-intuitive takeaways.
2. **Draft architecture figures**: When writing a key section (such as *request → retry → idempotent gateway*), ask the Agent to draft a hand-drawn flow on the Studio board.
3. **Refine & insert**: Adjust layout and sticky-note colors on the canvas, then insert the exported PNG right at your cursor.

## 2. Research Reading & Concept Mapping

1. **Capture paper notes**: Store structured paper notes in your vault linked with `[[Wikilinks]]`.
2. **Build comparison matrices**: Have the Agent read multiple paper notes together to summarize trade-offs into a structured table.
3. **Make logic visual**: Use Mind map or Flowchart templates on the Studio board to attach a visual summary at the top of a literature note.

## 3. Creator Long-Form & Visual Cards

1. **Distill key points**: Turn rough notes into structured arguments and memorable takeaways.
2. **Switch aspect ratio**: Set the Studio board to `3:4` or `9:16` and compose cards using sticky notes, hand-drawn icons, and device frames.
3. **Batch prep**: Use the **Image Toolbox** presets to resize or compress screenshots before publishing.
